1. About the Service & Wellness Disclaimer

Aura Drop is a hydration-tracking and habit-support application designed to help users establish and maintain healthy daily water intake habits through personalized targets, progress tracking, and scheduled reminders.

Health & Medical Disclaimer
Aura Drop is designed solely for general wellness and lifestyle tracking. It is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, treatment, or clinical recommendations. Hydration recommendations are general estimates based on user-provided inputs. Consult a qualified physician or healthcare professional regarding any medical condition, specialized fluid restrictions, or personal health requirements.

2. Information We Collect

We believe in data minimization. Aura Drop operates primarily on your device and collects only information necessary to deliver and personalize the Service.

2.1 Information You Provide Directly

Depending on the features you choose to use, you may enter:

  • Profile Information: Name or display name (optional).
  • Physical & Lifestyle Characteristics: Weight, height, biological sex, activity level, and general climate/temperature preference. This information is used strictly to calculate your recommended daily hydration targets.
  • Hydration & Intake Records: Amounts of water or beverages logged, drink types, timestamps of intake, and historical consumption records.
  • Schedule & Notification Preferences: Wake-up and bed times, preferred reminder frequency (cadence), and nighttime silent hours.
  • App Customizations: Preferred measurement units (Metric or Imperial) and audio reminder preferences.

You are never required to provide optional information to use the basic features of the application.

2.2 Device and Operational Information

When using the application, certain technical information may be processed by your device operating system to enable core functionality:

  • Device type, model, and operating system version.
  • Application version and build number.
  • System language, locale, and time zone settings.
  • Anonymous application-generated identifiers used strictly to manage local device storage partitions.

2.3 Device Permissions

Aura Drop may request the following permissions on your device:

  • Notifications: To deliver scheduled hydration reminders and motivational alerts. You can enable, disable, or adjust notifications at any time through your device settings or within the app.
  • Vibration / Haptics: To provide tactile feedback when confirming water entries or reminder alerts.

Aura Drop does not access your camera, microphone, photo library, contacts, location (GPS), financial accounts, or biometric sensors (such as Face ID or fingerprint data).

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information collected through Aura Drop solely for the following legitimate purposes:

  • Provide and Maintain the Service: Deliver hydration tracking, calculate daily hydration goals, and record beverage entries.
  • Deliver Scheduled Reminders: Calculate timely reminders aligned with your waking hours and chosen reminder intervals.
  • Display Progress & Trends: Generate daily, weekly, monthly, and yearly hydration charts, milestones, and streak analytics.
  • Personalize Experience: Remember your preferred measurement units, reminder cadences, and sound options.
  • Ensure Stability & Security: Maintain application integrity, troubleshoot crashes, and verify proper functionality.

We do not engage in automated user profiling, credit evaluation, or behavioral advertising.

4. Data Storage and Security

We are committed to protecting the security of your information:

  • On-Device Storage: Your hydration records, biometric inputs, and application preferences are stored directly on your personal device.
  • Security Measures: We apply industry-standard security and encryption practices to protect stored data against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.
  • No Central User Database: Aura Drop does not maintain a central cloud database containing your personal identity or hydration logs. Your data remains under your physical control on your device.

Please note that no method of electronic storage is 100% secure. While we implement standard commercial safeguards, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

5. Data Sharing and Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, monetize, or trade your personal information to third parties.

Information may only be shared under the following limited circumstances:

  • Device Operating System Services: If you choose to enable device-level backup services (such as Apple iCloud or Google Drive Backup), application data may be backed up in accordance with your device settings and the privacy policies of Apple or Google.
  • Legal Compliance: We may disclose information if required to do so by applicable law, regulation, subpoena, or lawful governmental request.

We do not integrate third-party advertising networks, analytics tracking brokers, or data syndicators into the application.

6. Your Rights, Choices & Data Deletion

You have complete control over your data:

  • Access & Review: You can review your hydration logs, daily averages, and profile settings directly within the application at any time.
  • Correction & Updates: You can modify your weight, activity level, climate, reminder times, and preferences through the Settings screen.
  • In-App Data Reset: You can permanently clear all historical hydration logs, intake records, and questionnaire settings by selecting Reset in the Settings screen.
  • Complete Deletion on Uninstall: Uninstalling Aura Drop from your device permanently removes all locally stored application data and configuration files.

7. Children's Privacy

Aura Drop is not directed to individuals under the age of 13 (or under 16 where required by local law). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you become aware that a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so that appropriate steps can be taken to delete the data.

8. International Data Transfers

Because your hydration records and profile details are stored locally on your device, your personal data is not transferred to remote overseas servers by Aura Drop. If operating system backups are utilized, data is managed according to your chosen cloud backup provider's international transfer practices.

When updates are made, we will revise the “Last Updated” date at the top of this policy. Continued use of Aura Drop after any revisions constitutes your acknowledgment of the updated Privacy Policy.
